# Privacy and editorial release checks

## Implemented behavior

- No GTM, Google Analytics, Meta script or tracking `noscript` beacon runs on first visit or after refusal. The old GTM container injected another Meta pixel and did not contain a GA measurement ID; it is removed. One existing Meta pixel is loaded only after explicit, versioned marketing consent. The previous generic `accepted` value is invalidated. Both choices are equally visible.
- The consent lasts at most 180 days and can be changed in the footer or privacy page. Withdrawal revokes the pixel, clears accessible optional cookies and reloads to unload previously executed code. Cross-tab withdrawals also stop it. Automatic Meta configuration and history tracking are disabled. Query/fragment URLs and purchase, administration, contact and interactive-tool pages do not load it. SPA navigation into a private flow unloads the already loaded pixel.
- reCAPTCHA, hosted fonts/images and infrastructure requests are separately described; rejecting marketing does not claim to eliminate required third-party services. The privacy page includes Groq, pseudonymous/public game data, order capabilities and actual retention behavior. It no longer promises unimplemented automatic 12-month deletion, 26-month analytics retention, or a universal five-year accounting period.
- Current self-assessment answers/results are calculated for the response without inserting them into the database or application logs. Existing protected records are not deleted by this change. Numerical weights, reverse-scored items, category boundaries and the absence of psychometric validation are disclosed. The outcome describes submitted answers; it is not a psychological profile or population percentile.
- The text analyzer separates untrusted input from system instructions, validates every score and explanation, requires exact source-text evidence for positive categories, and computes a disclosed author index. Invalid provider output is a service error, not a normal zero score. It does not perform fact-checking or establish author intent. The input and raw provider error are not logged or stored by the function. Groq's account-specific zero-retention configuration has not been verified.
- The game's likes and labels are explicitly described as simulated. Public texts no longer promise restored focus, hours saved or reduced anxiety. References distinguish research, conceptual frameworks and advocacy. Unrelated PMID 11548021 and PMC2825149 are removed; the re-identification link is corrected; references have usable author/year/title labels. Ego-depletion uncertainty, observational limits and lack of validation of the book are stated.
